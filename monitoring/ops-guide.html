Part 1 - Operation Rules

  1. Power schedule On: 0700 / Off: 12:01 (Managed by PoE Switch and PCU)
  2. Normal operating temperature range of AVoIP Devices (33°C - 45°C)
  3. Enforce a resolution of 1920x1080 at 50Hz for all PCs and AVoIP devices to ensure proper video output. A refresh rate of 60Hz will result in unstable video on Fujitsu Interactive TVs.
