Reading guide: Lighting and device status are measured data. Person counts cannot be determined automatically — the activity column reports the share of pixels that changed over 12 seconds. A room is Abnormal when a KDS reading is at/above 49°C, a device is unreachable, the camera is offline, or — in a dual / quad room — the displays are out of sync (they share one source, so every display should show the same picture). Out of sync is deliberately strict: it needs OCR / luminance evidence on both sides, a standby-vs-content mismatch, and a disagreeing area of at least 30% of the largest display; anything weaker is only a CHECK REGION note. Lighting never triggers an Abnormal verdict. A device counts as unreachable only on an explicit fail — a device still marked unknown has simply not been polled yet. Out of sync must repeat on two consecutive runs before it raises an Abnormal.
