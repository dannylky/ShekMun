How to read this
Lighting, device reachability and KDS temperature are measured values. A room is Abnormal only when a KDS reading is at or above 45°C, a display/AVoIP device is unreachable, or the camera is offline. Display screen state comes from OCR of each camera frame: a screen showing the AVoIP decoder standby banner or a Windows idle / lock screen (clock only, no application) is reported as Idle; anything else with readable content is Active. Picture-sync between displays still has to be confirmed by eye — open the attached AV_room_audit.html, where each room’s live frame sits inside its TV / LED wall cell. Person count is not available; the “Use” column is the share of pixels that changed over 12 seconds.
